# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leads, potential collaborators, and reviewers (including university / scholarship reviewers) who land on the site for 30–90 seconds and need to answer one question fast: *is this person worth contacting?* They scan on desktop and mobile, often from a link in a message or application. They are skeptical of junior-developer portfolios that overstate ability.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for **Abdulaziz Yusupaliev** — an AI Engineer & Frontend Developer from Tashkent. It exists to convert a quick visit into a credible impression and a contact action (email / GitHub / LinkedIn / Telegram). Success = the visitor leaves understanding his real stack, real shipped work (Hiwelcome.uz, Delever.uz, GradeFlow, ML notebook), and how to reach him — and remembers the site.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, cinematic. Voice is confident and clean, never cluttered. The visual world still borrows from Abdulaziz's own signal — the Vader mark and the `d_vaderrr` handle — but the blade has switched sides: a **twilight-teal developer identity**, a single soft aqua accent lighting a deep blue-slate near-dark (the light-side counterpart to the old Sith red). Open and relaxing, still cinematic. Three words: **composed, exact, alive.**

## Anti-references

- Generic dark SaaS portfolios: near-black + one timid gold/blue accent + Montserrat/Poppins + a fade-up on every section. The teal here is committed and the world is crafted (twilight gradients, aurora backdrop, varied reveals) — the opposite of a timid lone accent on flat black.
- Editorial-magazine cosplay (display-serif italics, drop caps, ruled three-column metadata) — not this brand.
- Overstated junior framing: fake metrics, "10x", hero-metric stat blocks, claims of work not actually done.
- Forced multi-second intros that replay on every visit.

## Design Principles

1. **Tell the truth, confidently.** Lead with what's real. AI Engineer ambition is stated, but proven frontend work carries the credibility. No invented numbers.
2. **One blade, much dark.** A single soft teal is the whole color story; everything else is deep blue-slate restraint. The accent earns its rarity.
3. **Contrast is non-negotiable.** Every text/background pair is verified readable. Low-opacity gray "for elegance" is banned.
4. **Motion with intent.** One orchestrated entrance and purposeful section reveals — never the same fade stamped on everything, never content gated behind an animation that can fail.
5. **Show the work, then get out of the way.** The fastest path from landing to "contact him" wins over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1, large/bold text ≥3:1, against actual backgrounds (this build verifies at ink 16:1, muted body 9.7:1, faint labels 6:1, teal accent 10.6:1, button ink 10.4:1). Full keyboard operability (nav, project drawer, mobile menu) with visible focus rings. Every decorative animation (intro, typed greeting, aurora backdrop, skill sphere, reveals) has a `prefers-reduced-motion` path. Semantic landmarks and one true `<h1>`. Teal is never the *only* signal for state — pair it with text, icon, or weight for color-blind safety.
